compass-rose

What this is

A thought record, shaped like a compass. Write down what you notice, one line at a time: the situation, thoughts, feelings, what you did. Connect what led to what, if you like, and try something different when you find yourself going round in circles.

What it isn't

Not a medical device, and it gives no clinical advice. The suggestions are optional. If you work with a therapist, their worksheet comes first.

Your data

Everything stays in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Copying an export is the only way anything leaves, and only when you do it.

Words

  • Entry: one line you write.
  • Connection: "this led to that". Optional.
  • Intervention: something you try: checking the evidence, a new thought, doing something different.
  • Resolve: wrap it up, with a note if you want one. Add anything afterwards and it's open again.
  • Needle: points at a corner you haven't looked at since your last intervention, or at the middle when you've gone round the loop.
