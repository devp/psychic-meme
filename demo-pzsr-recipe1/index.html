Supper Deck

Tonight's decisions

Newest first. Appended, never rebuilt.

What this is for

You already have the recipes. 115 of them, tagged by method, time and lane. The library isn't the problem — standing in the kitchen at 5:40 reading the library is the problem.

So the tonight tab shows one dish at a time. Swipe right or press Cook it and it lands on the first open night; swipe left and you never see it again this week. Narrow the pool with the chips before you start looking — that's the whole interaction model.

Three deliberate omissions

  • No recipe text. No steps, no quantities, no photos. You know how to cook these; that's why they're in the library. A card carries only what a decision needs.
  • No discovery. Nothing suggests new dishes, and nothing is trending. The library is a closed set you curate elsewhere.
  • No quantities on the list. Four dishes wanting onions is one line. How many onions is a judgement you make at the shelf; a number the app invented would be wrong with more confidence.

How the pieces connect

  • pantry holds every staple at have, low or out. low exists because it's the state a real kitchen is mostly in.
  • That feeds the deck: a card tells you what it needs that you haven't got, and the nothing to buy chip hides everything you can't cook tonight.
  • It also feeds shop, which is the union of what the picked nights need and what you've flagged anyway — grouped by store, because a flat list means re-sorting it in your head at every door.
  • Put away on the shop tab flips everything you ticked back to have. That's the loop that keeps the pantry honest.

Where things are

  • data/recipes.js, data/staples.js — the library and the tracked staples. Edit these first.
  • lib/plan.js — filtering, the deck shuffle, the shopping list. Pure functions; tests/plan.test.mjs covers them.
  • components/pick-deck.js — the swipe, in pointer events.
  • lib/store.js — localStorage with subscribers, so nothing goes stale.
  • lib/viewport.js — the mobile keyboard fix.
  • app.js — all the wiring, in the open.

No build step: the files you edit are the files that ship. Regenerate precache-manifest.js with just dev-rebuild after editing any of them.
