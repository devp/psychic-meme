About

An homage to a music concierge from the early 2010s that asked one good question: what are you doing right now? It answered with a few things that fit, picked by people, and then got out of the way.

Nothing here is real. The stations, the bands and the songs are invented, and nothing plays: the player only counts time. The interface is the point, and it will take any subject that fits the same three questions.
