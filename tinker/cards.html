Stickies

4

Open · Close 1 min each

Open

  1. Rubber band off.
  2. Next is the first ! sticky, else the first ?!, else the top ?.
  3. Read the goal cards in rank order, then the givens.

Close

  1. Write the next task as a ! sticky; put it on top of the stack.
  2. Rubber band.
  3. One log line saying where you stopped.

Coming back after weeks then takes 30 seconds. Pick one ritual per sitting; each fits a 20-minute burst.

5

Teach-sort 5 min · teaching order & cycles

  1. Shuffle the rule cards out face up.
  2. Repeat: put down any card whose needs: are all already in the row, preferring the one that comes earliest in play.
  3. If no card can be placed, the leftover cards that need each other form a cycle. Clip them together and put them in the row as one unit. Log cycle: a ↔ b.
  4. That's your run sheet: goal cards first (rank order), then givens, then the row. Photograph it.

A needs: name with no card turns up here: it can't be placed. That's also where typos surface, and where an attic dependency shows.

6

Goal trace 5 min · what each rule is for

  1. Goal cards across the top, in rank order.
  2. Under each goal, stack the rule and effect cards marked + for it.
  3. Givens in a second row, under the goals, above the stacks. Not columns: nothing has to stack under them, and a rule marked per a given still needs a goal.
  4. Pull in any card a stacked card needs, any card clipped behind one (sequences), and any card nested under one.
  5. Left on the table = doesn't trace to a goal. Give it a goal, connect it to something that has one, or attic it.
  6. A goal with an empty column has nothing serving it, unless it's ○ void.
leave it laid out for Tension7

Tension 3 min · cards still laid out from the goal trace

Tensions: + and −

Any card with both + and − goes sideways, spanning the two columns. Log each one, and say which goal wins. Rank decides by default.

Violations: ✗

Any card marked ✗ for a given is a violation, not a tension. Pull it out and put it on that given. Then either:

  • change the rule, or
  • re-make the given, and log ! saying why.
8

Readiness 2 min · tick, score in the log

  • pitch written (one line, on the box lid)
  • has goals
  • has rules
  • every name in a needs: exists as a card
  • no clipped cycles
  • every rule traces to a goal
  • every goal is served (or void)
  • every effect has needs: and a + or −
  • every stub has a slot, and its tables exist
  • no card breaks a given (✗)
  • no ?, ?!, or ! stickies on active cards

Settled ?! on the back of a given don't count.

score = ticks / 119

Paper playthrough 10–20 min

  1. Deal the teach-sort row.
  2. Walk one turn or scene in play order, reading each card aloud: "what happens here?"
  3. For a stub, roll on its tables and improvise.
  4. Anything unclear becomes a new sticky on that card.
  5. Anything improvised and worth keeping: log *, then move it → onto a card.
  6. An effect you watch happen: ☑ on its card, ★ in the log.

Attic · Migration · Whiteboard tinker · analog

Attic

Behind the ATTIC divider: never sorted, traced, or scored. If an active card needs one, the teach-sort shows it (the card isn't in the row): bring it forward or cut the dependency.

Migration (monthly, or when the box feels muddy)

Re-copy every active card by hand. Not worth re-copying → attic. Rewriting is where cards get merged, renamed, sharpened: the lint pass.

Whiteboard (optional)

Edges: solid → needs · dashed → +/− to goals · thick ⇒ sequences. Box each group or act. Sketches sit beside the cards. Photograph before erasing.
